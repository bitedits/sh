==== WHAT THIS LAYER COULD NOT SIZE ====
  jpl_word_pool (element 1028 bytes) has no capacity in the cap table.
     words are reachable from cmd nodes (Ext, For and Case patterns hold them),
         so they are bounded only by a PRODUCT of caps, and no entry of the LOCKED
         cap table is the capacity of a word.  The model has to add one (see the
         report's range) before this array can be sized; inventing a dimension here
         would be the parallel encoding this pipeline retires.
     What the locked caps DO imply, as a range of live words:
       lower       4096 words (one per cmd node)                = 4112.0 KiB
       upper    4194304 words (every node owning a full list)   = 4210688.0 KiB
     The model must fix one number in that range and prove it.  Nothing
     in the header allocates against a guess.
